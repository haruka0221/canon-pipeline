## Evidence records

### 41. W000031573 — *Snow Treasure*

- Current OL sources: `OL16057186W`, `OL18490202W`, `OL5844479W` (historical OL minimum edition year 1942).
- Evidence: Google Books records Marie McSwigan's *Snow Treasure*, E. P. Dutton, 1942, categorized as children's historical fiction. This is a single continuous narrative about Norwegian children during the German occupation.
- Provisional form/year/language: children's historical novel; 1942; English original indicated by US publication and author, pending any necessary first-edition verification.
- Candidate: `IN_SCOPE_CANDIDATE`. A child audience is **not** an exclusion criterion.
- Unresolved: independent examination of the three current OL Work source identities and their relation to the original edition; do not split or merge any Works here.
- Source: https://books.google.ca/books?id=pL4WAQAAMAAJ

### 42. W000022733 — *Red Hunters and the Animal People*

- Evidence: the first-edition text (Harper & Brothers, November 1904) has a table of contents listing independently titled animal/hunting stories; Open Library's associated 1904 edition describes twelve tales about Dakota beliefs and customs.
- Provisional form/year: multiple tales / Indigenous folklore and narrative collection; 1904.
- Candidate: `OUT_OF_SCOPE_CANDIDATE` by *work form* (not a single novel). This does not imply the constituent stories are irrelevant to other research.

### 43. W000007642 — *Mysteries of the Court of Napoleon III*

- Current OL source: `OL17699241W`; historical OL minimum edition year 1892.
- Evidence: the 1892 Chicago English-language edition, attributed to Gilbert Augustin Thierry, is catalogued with `E. I. R.` as translator and `M. A. B.` as joint translator. This is positive evidence of translation, distinct from title/author language inference.
- Candidate: `OUT_OF_SCOPE_CANDIDATE` provisionally, by translated manifestation. The source-language original, its title/date, and the narrative's fiction-versus-historical-prose form have **not** been independently established here.
- Outstanding: verify translators' credits against the book's title page and identify the underlying French source; confirm that `OL17699241W` refers to this edition, not just a homonymous text. If that link fails, reclassify as `UNRESOLVED`.
- Source (1892 digitized-copy metadata): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mysteries_of_the_court_of_Napoleon_III;_(IA_mysteriesofcourt00thie).pdf

### 44. W000024885 — *Wang the Ninth: The Story of a Chinese Boy*

- Current OL source: `OL6828096W`; historical OL minimum edition year 1920.
- Evidence: the original title page and copyright notice give Putnam Weale, Dodd, Mead & Company, New York, 1920. The English preface is dated Peking, July 1919; the book proceeds in third-person narrative chapters about Wang. The preface claims a degree of factual grounding, but this should not by itself turn a novelistic narrative into documentary non-fiction.
- Candidate: `IN_SCOPE_CANDIDATE` (1920 English-language single narrative). The precise genre and relation between the pseudonym Putnam Weale and B. L. Simpson can be confirmed before final adjudication.
- Source (original text): https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/37376/pg37376-images.html

### 45. W000029824 — *Torment* / *Better Angel*

- Current OL source: `OL77860W`; historical OL minimum edition year 1933.
- Evidence: Forman Brown's novel was first issued in **1933 as *Better Angel*** under the pseudonym Richard Meeker. The title ***Torment*** refers to a **1951** paperback retitling. An antiquarian catalogue of the 1951 edition explicitly links both editions and dates; the currently indexed Open Library edition list shows *Torment* under 1933, which must not be taken as a demonstrated 1933 *Torment* imprint.
- Candidate: `IN_SCOPE_CANDIDATE` **for the underlying 1933 English-language novel**, not for a supposed 1933 first edition of *Torment*.
- Outstanding: record the original-title/retitled-edition distinction; review the actual OL edition that generated the historical year rather than correcting the frozen corpus.
- Sources: https://www.burnsiderarebooks.com/pages/books/140945136/richard-meeker-forman-brown/torment-originally-published-as-better-angel ; https://openlibrary.org/books/OL1664266M/Better_angel

### 46. W000018488 — *The Assassins*

- Current OL source: `OL40935145W`; historical OL minimum edition year 1927.
- Evidence: a book review in *The Moslem World*, vol. 19 no. 1 (January 1929), p. 92, lists *The Assassins* by Sir Henry Sharp, Faber & Gwyer, London, 320 pp., and explicitly calls it **a novel** about the medieval Assassin sect. This independently supports the work form.
- Candidate: `IN_SCOPE_CANDIDATE`, subject to verifying the **first-edition year** (the 1927 OL-derived date has not been independently checked here), original composition language, and direct match to the OL Work.
- Source (contemporary journal review): https://www.kazima.org/media/Library/moslem-world-issues/muslim-world_1929-01_19_1.pdf
