### 22. W000026875 — *Armenian Romance*
- Historical Work: `OL7457410W`; historical linked-Edition minimum year: 1941.
- A seller describes a first edition published in Grand Rapids by Wm. B. Eerdmans in 1941, in English, and transcribes a title-page statement identifying the text as a true story, partly under fictitious, translated, or shortened names. Another bibliographic record gives a 1944 publication under the same publisher and author (a later manifestation may be involved).
- Publication during the target period is plausible, but a true account with substituted names is **not automatically a novel**, despite the word *Romance* and OL's fiction subject.
- **Proposal:** `UNRESOLVED` (`WORK_FORM_OR_FACTUALITY_UNRESOLVED`). Verify title page/preface and how the narrative is presented before assigning novel or fiction status. Do not treat the seller's transcription as independent primary inspection.
- Evidence: https://www.abebooks.com/first-edition/Armenian-Romance-Marie-Sarrafian-Banker-Eerdmans/30309224087/bd ; https://books.google.com/books/about/Armenian_Romance.html?id=Eho60gEACAAJ
