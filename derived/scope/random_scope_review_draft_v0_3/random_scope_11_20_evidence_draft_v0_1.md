1. Scope depends on **original conceptual-work publication**, language, and form, not the historical Edition-derived minimum date alone.
2. A translated English edition does not imply an English-original work. A short-story collection does not become a single novel because the database has one Work ID.
3. The draft primary-scope definition does **not** explicitly exclude juvenile fiction. Do not introduce that exclusion silently; identify children's fiction as a retained subclass pending confirmation.
