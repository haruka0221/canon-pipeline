You are resolving whether an Open Library work and a Goodreads work represent the same conceptual literary work.

Return exactly one decision:

MATCH
- The records represent the same conceptual work.
- Author name variants, initials, pseudonyms/real names, transliteration differences, translated titles, and edition-title differences may still be MATCH.

NO_MATCH
- The records represent different conceptual works.
- A shared or identical title alone is not sufficient.

RELATED_NOT_SAME_WORK
- The records are related but not the same conceptual work.
- Examples: adaptation, retelling, derivative work, anthology/collection containing the work, or one component of a multi-work volume.

AMBIGUOUS
- The supplied evidence is insufficient to decide reliably.

Important rules:
1. Judge conceptual work identity, not edition identity.
2. Publication years are supporting evidence only. Do NOT reject solely because years differ.
3. Open Library publication-year metadata may contain serious errors.
4. Goodreads original_publication_year may sometimes represent a later edition rather than the true original publication year.
5. Do not use ratings, popularity, or other visibility measures for identity resolution.
6. If the Goodreads contributor is a pseudonym, real name, spelling variant, transliteration, or abbreviated form of the Open Library author, this can support MATCH.
7. If titles match but the authors are genuinely different people, prefer NO_MATCH unless there is clear evidence of a pseudonym or another identity relationship.
8. If one record is an anthology, adaptation, retelling, or larger/smaller composite containing the other work, use RELATED_NOT_SAME_WORK rather than MATCH.
