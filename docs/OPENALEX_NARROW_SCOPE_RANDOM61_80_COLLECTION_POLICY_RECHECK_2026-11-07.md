## Working v0.2 eligibility definition (not yet approved as final release)

A separately identifiable original-English single novel/novella, or an original single-author short-fiction collection first assembled and published as such, with initial qualifying publication 1880–1950. Original collection year, first component-appearance year, original work year, first English translation year, first serialized appearance, and first complete book publication are distinct dates. Retain a novel/novella-only nested comparator. Children's novels are not disqualified. Individual short stories, genuinely multi-author anthologies, and nonfiction reference works are not admitted by the *single-author collection* expansion.

Mixed fiction + essays, adapted or novelized films, ambiguous collection-vs-component identity, substantial revisions/retitlings, and chronological boundary cases remain REVIEW, not automatic inclusion. Narrow OUT does not mean incorrectly collected in the historical broad English-edition corpus.

## Methodological limits and next review gate

- Completes **exploratory desk evidence coverage** for positions 1–80 (earlier checkpoints 1–60 and this 61–80), NOT any researcher adjudication. Existing 80-item sample remains frozen, 80 original researcher decision fields intentionally blank.
- Date anomalies (e.g., #74, #80), original-language translations (#65, #70), identity mismatches (#73, #77), movie tie-ins (#62), separate short stories (#78), and nonfiction/metadata contamination (#61, #64, #66) are independent QC dimensions, not interchangeable scope labels.
- No quantitative estimates of narrow eligibility in the complete 32,066 W are warranted from AI-only judgments; statuses are conditional on human examination of evidence, identity, work form, original language and first publication.
- Clarify `MIXED_FORM_SINGLE_AUTHOR_COLLECTION` and original short-fiction collections vs retrospective anthologies, and decide how self-contained novelizations relate to the primary research question. Publish reviewed membership as a **separate versioned layer** only after researcher approval; join back to frozen OpenAlex v3, retaining all W and NA/zero distinctions.
- The original broader corpus is for translation/reprint/English circulation analysis. A novel that fails the original-English constraint is not automatically an incorrectly collected record; false historic years and nonfiction catalogs are distinct provenance/genre issues.
