## Scope rule

- Broad historical English-edition / circulation corpus can retain translations, older novels republished in 1880–1950, and collections under the documented collecting rules; their inclusion is not evidence that they qualify for the narrow analysis.
- Narrow OpenAlex primary analysis requires an individual novel or novella originally composed in English and originally first published 1880–1950 inclusive; children's/YA standalone novels may qualify. The final policy for serial dates, individual short stories and the novella boundary remains open.
- Never convert edition-derived OL dates, source subject flags, Wikidata statements, AI judgments, or bibliographic source alerts directly into formal `IN_SCOPE`/`OUT_OF_SCOPE`.
- No source Work, Project W, OpenAlex, Goodreads, researcher-review columns, or holdout file is changed here.
