## The working v0.2 rule

For the OpenAlex primary analytical population, consider English-original novels/novellas with original publication in 1880–1950, plus *identifiable original single-author short-fiction collections* first formed/published during that interval. Record original-language, first original publication, collection-first publication and any version/retitling separately. Preserve a nested novel/novella-only comparator. Adult vs juvenile readership does not alone determine scope. A single-author volume of political satire, newspaper columns, reportage or factual sketches does not necessarily meet a short-*fiction* collection criterion. This is a tentative review policy, not an approved release.

The broad historical English-edition corpus may legitimately contain pre-1880 originals republished in the target years and non-English originals translated into English then; narrow exclusion is not a general data-cleaning instruction. Out-of-period originals erroneously assigned an 1880–1950 OL date and nonfiction reference lists are separate bibliographic QC issues.

## Issues to feed into the reproducible protocol

1. The new collection rule needs an explicit treatment of literary essay/satire columns, recurrent fictional characters, and volumes that have both narrated fiction and overt nonfiction exposition. Default: `MIXED_FORM_REVIEW` unless sufficient genre evidence supports classification as a short-fiction collection. Treat *Mr. Dooley* and earlier *Reginald in Russia*, *Red Hunters*, *Ginger* as distinct illustrations of why there is no safe one-flag inclusion.
2. An edition's title-page year need not equal the *first edition's* year: *Mr. Dooley* contains an unambiguous November 1898 first-edition statement despite 1899 on the digitized copy's title page.
3. The 1924 entries for *Double Trouble Squared* are bibliographic anomalies. They should be flagged for source provenance investigation separately from novel form or original English language.
4. Coauthored novels (*Mr. Popper's Penguins*) remain eligible; the proposed collection subgroup's one-author definition is a different policy choice.
5. External source URLs and existing OL match decisions still require object-level researcher verification. No `IN_SCOPE` /`OUT_OF_SCOPE` layer, OpenAlex correlations, or original dataset alterations are authorized by this review.
