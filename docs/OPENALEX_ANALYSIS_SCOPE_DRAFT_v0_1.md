# OpenAlex Primary Analysis Scope — Draft v0.1

Date: 2026-10-20
Status: DRAFT — methodological working decision, not adjudicated scope release

## 1. Primary analysis population

The primary OpenAlex scholarly-visibility analysis will focus on
individual works of fiction originally written in English and first
published between 1880 and 1950 (inclusive).

- Novels and novellas are provisionally included.
- Poetry, drama, anthologies, and multi-work collections are excluded
  from the primary analysis.
- Works originally published before 1880 are excluded even if later
  editions appeared during 1880–1950.
- Translations of works originally written in other languages are
  excluded from this primary analysis.

Still unresolved:
- Treatment of individual short stories.
- Publication-year convention for serialized fiction.
- Borderline genre cases and uncertain bibliographic evidence.

## 2. Historical corpus versus analytical scope

The historical Open Library population of 34,789 source records is
not assumed to be a validated corpus of 1880–1950 English novels.

Its historical date evidence is derived from linked Editions and
does not necessarily represent original work publication dates.

The broader bibliographic population and existing Project W identities
will be retained. They may be used in publication, circulation,
translation, reception, and educational analyses.

The above restriction applies specifically to the primary OpenAlex
analysis, not automatically to every dissertation analysis.

## 3. Scope evidence

For each reviewed Project W, distinguish:

- Original work publication year
- First English manifestation year
- Original language
- Work form (novel, novella, story, collection, etc.)
- Analysis-scope status
- Bibliographic evidence and uncertainty

Insufficient evidence must remain unresolved.

Scope decisions must not silently change Project W identity.

## 4. OpenAlex data preservation

Retain integrated OpenAlex visibility v2 unchanged.

Its 226,221 operational candidate pairs are not equivalent to
226,221 verified scholarly references.

Preserve distinctions between:
- Works with operational candidates
- Queried works with zero operational candidates
- 352 active W without R2/R3 queries
- One unremeasured Alcott W (NA)

Target-attribution precision and cross-W candidate overlap remain
separate QC questions.

## 5. Evidence pilot and next steps

Non-adjudicative scope evidence pilot v1:
derived/scope/scope_evidence_pilot_v1/

Pilot: 159 unique W, including an independent random sample of 80 W.

Purposively selected cases and random cases must not be pooled
to estimate population-wide scope error rates.

Next:
1. Establish consistent bibliographic evidence and review rules.
2. Test them on the initial 13 high-information W.
3. Keep unresolved cases explicitly unresolved.
4. Evaluate the independent random sample separately.
5. Produce a new, versioned analytical-scope release only after review.

No existing OpenAlex v2, identity, frozen evaluation, or completed
Edition-audit artifact is modified by this draft.
